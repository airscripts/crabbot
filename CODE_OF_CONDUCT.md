# Code of Conduct

Crabbot follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct.html).
Participate with empathy, respect, and care. Harassment, threats, personal
attacks, discrimination, and publishing private information are not accepted.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[francesco@airscript.it](mailto:francesco@airscript.it). Reports are reviewed
promptly and confidentially.
